This policy explains what happens to the information you share when you use the contact form, the quote request form, or otherwise get in touch through this website.

## What information is collected

When you submit the Contact or Get a Quote form, the following may be collected: your name, email address, and the details you choose to share about your project or enquiry.

## How it is used

This information is used only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, or deliver the service you've requested. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Form handling

Form submissions on this site are processed through [Netlify Forms — confirm and update this section once hosting is finalized]. Submitted data is stored by that service in order to deliver it to the site owner.

## Confidentiality of project materials

Documents or materials shared as part of a translation, writing, or other project are treated as confidential, consistent with the practice described on this site's Services pages.

## Your rights

You can ask, at any time, what information is held about you and request that it be corrected or deleted. Contact details are available on the Contact page.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent update.
