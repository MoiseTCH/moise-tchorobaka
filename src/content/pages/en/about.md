I'm Moïse Yoro Tchorobaka, a language professional specializing in bilingual English-French translation and French-as-a-foreign-language teaching. I hold a professional degree (licence professionnelle) in bilingual English-French translation, and I've built my career around languages, communication, education, and passing on knowledge.

## Background

My training in translation gave me strong comprehension and expression skills in both languages, along with close attention to precision, clarity, and staying true to meaning. My professional experience in translation also gave me a real understanding of what bilingual communication demands — and of how content needs to adapt to its context and audience.

I currently teach French at an international school, working with English-speaking learners on vocabulary, grammar, listening and reading comprehension, and spoken and written expression. My bilingual English-French background is a particular asset here: I understand the specific difficulties learners run into moving between the two languages, and I use that understanding to explain the grammatical, lexical, and structural differences between English and French clearly.

## How I teach

Learning a language should mean being able to communicate, understand, and actually use it in real situations. That's why I put real weight on student participation, regular practice, and adapting activities to each learner's level — bringing in visual materials, hands-on activities, learning games, and digital tools along the way.

## Looking ahead

I'm also interested in developing educational resources and in how digital tools and AI can support education — how they can help create better-suited teaching materials and make producing quality educational content easier.

## Let's work together

If you're looking for translation, language coaching, writing help, a website, or AI training, I'd be glad to hear what you're working on.
