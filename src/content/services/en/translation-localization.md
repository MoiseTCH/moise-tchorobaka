Whether it's a single contract or an entire website, translation only works when it reads as though it were written in the target language from the start. That's the difference between translation and localization, and it's the standard every project here is held to.

## Background

Translation work here is grounded in a professional degree (licence professionnelle) in bilingual English-French translation, built specifically around accuracy, clarity, and staying true to meaning — not general bilingualism applied to a task it wasn't trained for.

## What's included

Document translation, website translation, content translation, proofreading, editing, and full localization — covering both English-to-French and French-to-English, for individuals and businesses alike.

## Confidentiality

Every document is treated as confidential by default, whether it's a business contract or a personal record.
