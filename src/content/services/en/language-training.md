Learning a language works best when the lessons are built around what you actually need it for — not a generic syllabus. Whether that's holding a conversation, passing an exam, or sounding confident in a professional setting, the plan starts with your goal.

## Background

This isn't a side hobby — French teaching is active, day-to-day work. Currently teaching French at an international school to English-speaking learners means constant, direct experience with exactly the kind of difficulties an English speaker runs into learning French: where the grammar, vocabulary, and sentence structure genuinely diverge between the two languages, and how to explain that clearly.

## What's included

French lessons, English lessons, English as a Foreign Language (EFL) support, conversation practice, and fully personalized language training — for adult learners and working professionals alike.
