AI tools are genuinely useful, but only once you know what they're actually good at — and where a human still needs to check the work, especially anything involving language. This is training grounded in that distinction, not just a tour of features.

## Background

This comes from active, ongoing interest — exploring how digital tools and AI can create better-suited teaching materials and make producing quality educational content easier, applied directly in a real classroom setting rather than studied in the abstract.

## What's included

AI tools training, AI productivity training, AI-assisted content creation guidance, and practical sessions for educators and professionals getting started with AI.

*This is a newer part of the practice — sessions are hands-on and shaped around what you're actually trying to do, rather than a fixed curriculum.*
